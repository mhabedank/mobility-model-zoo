# Contract: generated documents

What each generated document must contain. All documents are rendered from the register by `zoo compliance render` and checked for drift in CI. All are in English and name no data subject.

| File | Must contain |
|---|---|
| `PRIVACY.md` | Who is responsible: controller and contacts from `controller.yaml`, with links to the imprint and website privacy policy. What the project does, as the purposes: building a benchmark, training data, evaluation, publication of models. The legal basis per source class: Art. 6(1)(f) with a summary of the legitimate-interest assessment, and Art. 9(2)(e) for parliamentary records. The source classes with examples of publishers (no individuals). Recipients, taken from `recipients.yaml`, each with route, provider, region and retention as recorded, including `unknown` and past unpinned calls. Retention per class. That datasets and training texts are not published. Rights under Art. 15–18 and 20, the Art. 21 objection right as its own section, the right to complain with the authority named, and how to send a request (e-mail; the GitHub template only for non-personal requests). Last updated date. |
| `COPYRIGHT_POLICY.md` | The crawler user agent and contact URL. The legal basis (§44b UrhG; official works §5). The signals honoured: robots.txt including the AI user agents, TDMRep, `noai`/`X-Robots-Tag`, ai.txt, and plain-language terms (D6). That login, paywalls and CAPTCHAs are never circumvented. That deny and piracy lists are applied. Retention and deletion. The takedown process and deadline. That training texts are not redistributed. The verbatim limit for published files. |
| `SECURITY.md` | How to report a vulnerability or misuse (e-mail), and what is in scope (code, firmware, model cards). That the models are research artefacts, not safety mechanisms. |
| `NOTICE` | Project name and licence. Third-party notices required by Apache-2.0 §4(d) and by MIT/BSD dependencies that are redistributed. The XLM-R MIT copyright notice for models fine-tuned from it. |
| `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` | Per source or dataset used by a published model: title, creators, source link, licence with link, modifications, and attribution text (TASL). Per redistributed dependency: name, licence, copyright. |
| `REUSE.toml`, `LICENSES/` | Licence for every file (globs for generated and data-free folders); `reuse lint` passes. |
| `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/rights-request.yml` | For opt-out and takedown of works (no personal data). It points to the privacy e-mail for anything personal. |
| `docs/compliance/record-of-processing.md` | The Art. 30 record: purposes, categories of data subjects and data, recipients, transfers, retention and measures, per source class. |
| `docs/compliance/lia-dpia.md` | The legitimate-interest assessment per source class (D10) and a short DPIA: description, necessity, risks, measures, residual risk (D11). |
| `zoo/models/<m>/releases/<v>.ai-act.md` | The classification record in prose, with the legal references and their dates. |
| `zoo/models/<m>/releases/<v>.training-data-summary.md` | The EU AI Office template structure: general information; data sources by category (public datasets, licensed data, web-scraped data with the top domains, synthetic labels with the teacher models); processing (opt-out handling, removal of illegal content, PII redaction). |
| Model card sections | "Training data and attribution" (TASL table, modification notes, NOTICE text). "Teacher and labeling models" (route, hosting provider, terms checked at). "Out-of-scope use". "Dual-use considerations" (security topics). "Privacy and personal data" (what was done, link to `PRIVACY.md`, how to object). Links to `COPYRIGHT_POLICY.md` and the AI Act record at the release tag. |
