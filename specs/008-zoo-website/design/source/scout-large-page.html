Interface

One text in, a list of spans out.

Input

One UTF-8 text per call, German or English: an interview, a forum post, a review, a passage from a study. The reference size for the speed figures is a 9,240-character interview.

Format versioning

The output format is jtbd-span-v1. Fields are not renamed or removed within a format version; a breaking change ships as a new format name together with a new model version.

Intended use and limits

What it is for, and where to stop trusting it.

Intended use

  • Finding candidate jobs, pains and gains in German and English texts about mobility.
  • Pointing a researcher to the passage, so the quote can be read in its context.
  • Running locally on texts that must not leave the machine.

Out of scope

  • Drawing conclusions without reading the quoted passages.
  • Profiling or scoring individual people.
  • Other languages or other domains, unless you evaluate them yourself first.
Limitations

The composite is agreement with two frontier reference models on a frozen benchmark, not accuracy against human labels. Speeds are for a 9,240-character interview on the named hardware and will differ on other texts and machines. [Further limitations from the model card.]
