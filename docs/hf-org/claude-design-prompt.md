Design five logo proposals for **Mobility Model Zoo**, an open collection of small, fast machine learning models for the mobility sector (public transport, cycling, shared and on-demand mobility). The models run locally on ordinary hardware. Every model is measured on a fixed benchmark, versioned and published on Hugging Face with an honest model card. The first models extract customer needs (jobs-to-be-done, pains, gains) from interviews and reviews, and later topics will add more.

**Where the logo is used**
- Primarily as the Hugging Face organization avatar: a square shown with rounded corners, often only 32 to 48 px wide, next to other orgs' logos.
- Later on a GitHub README header and on slides, so it needs a mark on its own and a version with the wordmark "Mobility Model Zoo".

**What it should convey**
Movement and routes (mobility), something small, light and fast, and a calm, technical kind of trust. It should look like a modern tech brand (think Linear, Mapbox, Vercel, Hugging Face) and not like a transit-agency app or a clip-art icon. Abstract is welcome.

**What we rejected so far, and why**
- A transit line with a dense cluster of crossed network nodes: detail was unbalanced, with one part very fine and the rest very coarse.
- A fox head made of route lines: too busy at small sizes.
- A neural-network "wheel": generic, with no recognizable mobility.
- A map pin with a bus: looks like a public-transport app, not like ML.
- A ring crossed by a diagonal line: reads as "Ø" or a prohibition sign.
- The current shortlist is two parallel S-shaped route lines with one station dot, and three speed lines with a ring. They are acceptable but not yet distinctive. Feel free to beat them.

**Hard constraints**
- One consistent stroke weight or shape language across the whole mark; no mix of fine and coarse detail.
- At most three elements and two or three colors; no gradients, shadows, 3D or text inside the mark.
- Optically centered and balanced in the square, with generous padding (the avatar is cropped with rounded corners).
- Must stay recognizable at 32 px and work on both a dark and a light background.
- Avoid clichés: no brain, no circuit board, no generic network graph, no car silhouette, no map pin, no animal mascot.

**Color**
Propose a modern mobility palette per concept, for example electric blue, mint or signal green, deep indigo or navy, with one warm accent at most. Give the hex values and check that the contrast between mark and background is strong.

**Deliverables for each of the five proposals**
1. A name and one sentence on the idea behind it.
2. The mark as clean SVG code (viewBox 0 0 512 512, geometric primitives, background as a full-bleed rect).
3. A preview grid: 512 px, 48 px and 32 px, each on dark and on light, plus the mark next to the wordmark "Mobility Model Zoo" set in a clean sans-serif.
4. The palette as hex values.
5. An honest weakness: where it could be confused with something else or fail.

Make the five proposals clearly different from each other, not five colorways of one idea. End with a short ranking and your recommendation.
